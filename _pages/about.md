### About Me

I am **Ali M’amanpoosh**, a computer engineering student at the University of Isfahan (B.Sc. 2020–2024). My passion lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence and software engineering. I am particularly interested in AI‑enabled software engineering, large language models and vision–language models, retrieval‑augmented generation, and semantic analysis of technical text.

### Research Journey

During my studies, I have been fortunate to work on a variety of research projects. Under the guidance of **Prof. Afsaneh Fatemi** at the University of Isfahan, I explored bilingual 3D visual reasoning and physics‑aware vision–language modelling and designed privacy‑preserving mechanisms for retrieval‑augmented generation systems. As part of a collaboration with **Prof.dr Anna Fensel** at Wageningen University & Research, I have been developing text‑to‑RDF conversion and validation methods for soil health knowledge graphs. I also worked with **Dr Hossein Karshenas** on semantic analysis of long‑form technical text for IoT fault diagnosis and with **Dr Mahmudreza Changizian** on computer vision for safety‑critical systems. My earlier research at the Institute for Research in Fundamental Sciences (IPM) involved designing an eye‑tracking system to monitor behavior and neuron activity.

### Teaching & Professional Experience

I enjoy sharing my knowledge with others and have served as a teaching assistant for a range of courses at the University of Isfahan, including Natural Language Processing, Theory of Automata, Foundations of Artificial Intelligence, Human–Computer Interaction, UI Design, Advanced Programming, Object‑Oriented Analysis and Design, and Formal Languages and Automata. Professionally, I work as a **freelance machine learning engineer**, developing AI solutions such as chatbots and custom tools for code and text processing. Previously, I worked as a **QML programmer at BEHYAAR Co.**, where I designed user interfaces for aircraft systems using QML and C++.

### Beyond Academia

Outside of research and teaching, I contribute to the community through **TEDx University of Isfahan**, where I served as head of the logistics team and as a logistics team member in previous years. I continuously advance my skills through courses and certifications in machine learning and large language models. I speak native **Persian** and am proficient in **English**.
